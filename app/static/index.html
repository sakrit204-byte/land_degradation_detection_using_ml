      About CARE-EW

      CARE-EW (Climate-Adjusted Residual Early Warning) estimates, for every 10 m pixel, the probability that a persistent vegetation decline that cannot be explained by rainfall, temperature or soil moisture will begin within the next 6 months.

      • Risk – calibrated probability of a persistent degradation onset within 6 months; colour scale anchored on the model's operating threshold (shown from half the threshold upward). Purple hatching = conformal abstain: inspect in the field.
      • NDVI anomaly – standardised departure from the 2017–2020 seasonal baseline.
      • Hotspots – boxes around connected high-risk clusters at the latest month.
      • Rivers – HydroRIVERS network draining into the AOI; discharge change from GloFAS (1984–2000 vs 2016–).

      Data sources

      Copernicus Sentinel-2 L2A (ESA) · ESA WorldCover · HydroSHEDS / HydroRIVERS (WWF) · GloFAS (Copernicus Emergency Management Service) · ERA5-Land climate · Base imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics · OpenStreetMap contributors.

      Probabilities are EO-proxy based and not field-verified.
